The story
She doesn't expect to fall first.

When Win, a flirty 25 year old tennis player, bets she can make Kunle, a stoic 50 year old widower, fall for her, she doesn't expect to fall first. Or to discover he's her estranged father's business partner.

She picks her mark.

Who's in it
Two people, one bet.
Meet Win

Win Adenuga played by Ruby Okezie

25, tennis player, used to winning

Win says yes to a dare in front of her friends: she can make Kunle fall for her. She picks him out of a crowd because he is the only man in the room not looking at her. She has three months and a group chat watching.

What she does not know yet is whose business partner he is, or that she is the one who will not walk away clean.

Meet Kunle

Kunle played by Ramsey Nouah

50, widower, not playing

Kunle buried his wife four years ago and has not been interested in being interesting since. He is courteous, unmoved and completely unaware that he is the subject of a bet.

He is also the one person Win cannot afford to fall for. Made in collaboration with Ramsey Nouah Production.
